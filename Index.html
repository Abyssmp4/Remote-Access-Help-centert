Wi-Fi Troubleshooting

Wi-Fi Connection

Click To Troubleshoot

Troubleshooting steps

  1. Check if your Wi-Fi is turned on.
  2. Turn Wi-Fi off and on again.
  3. Make sure airplane mode is off.
  4. Find a spot closer to the router, for a stronger signal.
  5. Restart your device.

Contact IT support if the issue persists.

Can't Connect to Wi-Fi

Click To Troubleshoot

Troubleshooting steps

  1. Make sure you select the correct Wi-Fi network.
  2. Carefully re-enter your Wi-Fi password.
  3. Forget the Wi-Fi network and re-add it.
  4. Turn Wi-Fi off and on again.
  5. Restart your device.

Contact IT support if the issue persists.

Connected to Wi-Fi but No Internet

Click To Troubleshoot

Troubleshooting steps

  1. Try accessing a website in a different browser.
  2. Disconnect and reconnect to the Wi-Fi network.
  3. Restart your device.
  4. Check if another device can connect to the internet.
  5. Restart you router/modem

Contact IT support if the issue persists.

VPN Troubleshooting

VPN Won't Connect

Click To Troubleshoot

Troubleshooting steps

  1. Make sure you're have a stable Wi-Fi connection.
  2. Close the VPN client and reopen it.
  3. Verify that your choosing the correct VPN server.
  4. Try connecting from a different location or device.
  5. Restart your device.

Contact IT support if the issue persists.

VPN Login or Authentication Issues

Click To Troubleshoot

Troubleshooting steps

  1. Re-enter your username and password.
  2. Make sure caps lock is off.
  3. Check your internet connection.
  4. Check to make sure your account is active and not locked.
  5. Try connecting from a different location or device.

Contact IT support if the issue persists.

VPN Connection Drops Frequently

Click To Troubleshoot

Troubleshooting steps

  1. Check your internet connection and make sure it's stable.
  2. Move closer to the Wi-Fi router, if possible.
  3. Close any other applications that might be using the internet.
  4. Try disconnecting and reconnecting to the VPN.
  5. Restart your device and the VPN service.

Contact IT support if the issue persists.

Slow or dropped connection troubleshooting

Slow Connection Speed

Click To Troubleshoot

Troubleshooting steps

  1. Close any unnecessary applications or browser tabs.
  2. Check for any downloads or uploads that might be using the internet.
  3. Move closer to the Wi-Fi router, if possible.
  4. Disconnect and reconnect to the Wi-Fi network.
  5. Restart your device.

Contact IT support if the issue persists.

Internet Keeps Disconnecting

Click To Troubleshoot

Troubleshooting steps

  1. Check your Wi-Fi signal strength and move closer to the router if possible.
  2. Turn Wi-fi off and on again.
  3. Restart your device, and router if you have access to it.
  4. Check if other devices are experiencing similar issues.
  5. Check for outages in your area.

Contact IT support if the issue persists.

High Latency or Ping Issues

Click To Troubleshoot

Troubleshooting steps

  1. Close any downloads, streams, or other bandwidth-intensive applications.
  2. Move closer to the Wi-Fi route or use a wired connection if possible.
  3. Disconnect any devices that might be using a lot of bandwidth that are not in use.
  4. Restart your device and router if you have access to it.
  5. Check for any network congestion or issues in your area.

Contact IT support if the issue persists.
